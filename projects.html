Projects

Current and developing research

Grounding Financialization

Dissertation · tenant struggles and the politics of housing finance

Three years of embedded ethnographic fieldwork with a national tenant movement, alongside archival and policy analysis, examining how rental housing financialization structures tenants' efforts to govern their housing — and how they reach federal institutions like Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. I conceptualize housing finance as a spatial infrastructure, comparing today's movement with the Civil Rights–era National Tenant Organization (1969–1975). Supported by the Sagalyn-Hack Dissertation Research Award.

Counter-Histories of Grassroots Planning

Memory, Black geographies, and Washington Park, Chicago

“Left at the Altar of Development” reconstructs a tradition of Black grassroots spatial production amid racial violence, segregation, and institutional abandonment, analyzing a corpus of Chicago Defender articles. Drawing on Black geographies, I write against representations of the neighborhood as vacant and disordered. Supported by the MIT Racially Just Research Initiative; draft complete, submission to Urban Studies planned for Summer 2026.

Sites of Transition

Post-industrial memory and the siting of the digital economy

A developing project extending my dissertation from housing to infrastructure, centered on a quantum computing facility planned for the former South Works steel plant site on Chicago's far South Side. I examine how residents mobilize place-based knowledge, oral history, and memory of environmental harm to negotiate the stakes of redevelopment — and how temporal framing (the promise of “future innovation”) operates as a governance strategy.

Data, Platforms, and Housing Markets

Participatory action research · computational and spatial analysis

How data infrastructures and digital platforms reshape housing advocacy and markets. A co-authored article in the Journal of Urban Affairs on advocates' use of public data; a Harvard Joint Center–supported working paper on real estate platforms, presented at the 2024 Project REACh Financial Inclusion Summit; and participatory parcel-level analysis of rental ownership with the Healthy Neighborhoods Study.
