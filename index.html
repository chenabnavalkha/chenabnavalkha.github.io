About Research Teaching CV

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Cities and Communities at the Johns Hopkins University School of Government and Policy. I am also a Research Affiliate of the Political Economy Lab at MIT and a Good Life Fellow at the Roosevelt Institute.

My research examines how financialization and the digital economy shape housing, land, and urban development—and how communities mobilize to negotiate and influence the institutions governing everyday life. Drawing on historical, ethnographic, spatial, and community-engaged approaches, I connect large-scale political and economic shifts to their consequences for cities and communities. I seek to understand how urban change is governed and to generate knowledge that supports more equitable, sustainable, and accountable approaches to urban planning and policy.

I hold a Ph.D. in Urban and Regional Planning and a Master in City Planning from MIT, as well as a B.A. in Philosophy and Biology from the University of Chicago.
