About Research Teaching CV

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Cities and Communities at the Johns Hopkins University School of Government and Policy. I am also a Research Affiliate of the Political Economy Lab at MIT and a Good Life Fellow at the Roosevelt Institute.

My research examines how financialization and the digital economy are transforming housing, land, and urban development—and how communities organize to shape the institutions and decisions affecting their lives. Through historical, ethnographic, spatial, and community-engaged research, I connect large-scale shifts in political economy to their consequences in cities and communities. I also study how residents and community organizations produce knowledge, build collective capacity, and contest the forces shaping their communities. My broader aim is to advance more equitable, sustainable, and accountable approaches to urban development and public policy.

I hold a Ph.D. in Urban and Regional Planning and a Master in City Planning from MIT, as well as a B.A. in Philosophy and Biology from the University of Chicago.
