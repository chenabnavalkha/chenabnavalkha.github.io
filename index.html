About Research Teaching CV

I am an incoming Postdoctoral Fellow in Cities and Communities at the Johns Hopkins School of Government and Policy. I am also a Research Affiliate of the Political Economy Lab at MIT and a Good Life Fellow at the Roosevelt Institute.

My research examines how finance, property, and infrastructure shape urban development and inequality. I take an interdisciplinary approach, working at the intersection of urban planning, geography, political economy, and community-engaged research. I study how changing systems of housing, land, and urban development reshape cities and the lived experience of inequality, and how residents and community organizations understand and shape these transformations. My work spans rental housing and housing finance, land use and redevelopment, digital infrastructures, and community planning, with attention to local context and histories of racialization in access to urban space.

I hold a Ph.D. and Master’s in City Planning from MIT and a B.A. in Philosophy and Biology from the University of Chicago. Before graduate school, I worked as a public health researcher and patient educator at the University of Chicago.
